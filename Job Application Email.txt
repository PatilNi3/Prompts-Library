# AI Prompt: Professional Job Inquiry / Application Email

Act as a senior technical recruiter and professional communication writer specializing in Data Engineering hiring.

I am a Data Engineer exploring opportunities. I've found a Data Engineer role at a company whose JD appears to match my background. Help me analyze the fit and write a targeted outreach email.

## Step 0 — Gather Context First
Before writing anything, ask me for whatever of this you don't already have:
1. The company's Job Description (JD)
2. My resume
3. Company name
4. Recruiter/HR contact name, if known (otherwise use "Hiring Team")
5. How I found the role: job board / company careers page / referral / recruiter outreach / other
6. If referred: who referred me and my relationship to them
7. One thing specific to this company worth referencing (product, recent news, mission, tech stack) — if I don't have one, note that the email will lean on role/JD alignment instead of a company-specific hook

Do not proceed to Step 1 until you have the JD and resume at minimum.

## Step 1 — Analyze the Job Description
Identify: most important technical skills, required cloud/platform technologies, programming languages, data engineering tools, years of experience required, domain requirements, key responsibilities, preferred/nice-to-have skills.

## Step 2 — Analyze My Resume
Identify the strongest, most relevant experience matching the JD. Prioritize (in this order, only where the resume actually supports it): Azure, Azure Databricks, PySpark, Apache Spark, Spark SQL, SQL, Delta Lake, Azure Data Factory, ADLS Gen2, Lakehouse Architecture, metadata-driven architecture, CDC/incremental processing, data quality, ETL/ELT, Unity Catalog, CI/CD, healthcare data engineering, and measurable achievements.

**Do NOT mention any technology, tool, or experience not explicitly present in my resume.**

## Step 3 — Identify the Strongest Match
Determine the 3–5 strongest alignment points between my experience and the JD. Weave these in naturally rather than copying resume bullets. Prioritize measurable, production-scale achievements (e.g., data volume processed, table/source counts, records processed, % automation, % reduction in manual effort, % code reusability, % reduction in development time) — **only using metrics that literally appear in the resume I provide.**

**Match-strength gate:** If overall alignment is below roughly 60%, say so explicitly before drafting, and ask whether I still want an email written (and if so, whether to frame it around transferable skills rather than direct alignment) rather than silently writing an over-enthusiastic email for a weak fit.

## Step 4 — Write the Email
Write a concise, professional, recruiter-friendly email that:
- States interest in the specific Data Engineer role at the named company
- Reflects how I found the role (referral mention if applicable — naming the referrer; otherwise how I came across the posting)
- Weaves in one company-specific detail from Step 0 if provided, so it doesn't read as a template
- Briefly highlights relevant technical expertise matched to the JD
- Mentions 2–3 highly relevant, resume-supported achievements
- States total years of experience
- Notes resume is attached
- Ends with a clear, professional call to action

### Writing Requirements
- ~150–200 words for a standard email; ~100–130 words if this is a LinkedIn/InMail-style message instead of formal email — ask which channel if unclear
- Professional but natural tone; no desperation, no "perfect candidate" claims, no generic filler
- No copying large resume blocks; no listing every skill I have — only JD-relevant ones
- No exaggeration or unsupported claims
- No mention of ATS or resume optimization
- Subject line specific to the role and company

### Structure
```
Subject: Application / Interest – Data Engineer | [Most Relevant Skill] | [Company]
Dear [Recruiter/HR Name or Hiring Team],

Opening: brief self-intro + interest in this specific role, referencing how I found it.
Relevant Match: why the JD aligns with my background — key technologies + responsibilities, with the company-specific hook woven in naturally.
Key Experience: 2–3 measurable, resume-backed achievements.
Closing: resume attached, interest in discussing further, clear CTA.

Regards,
Nitin Patil
Data Engineer
[Phone]
[Email]
[LinkedIn – if provided]
```

## Step 5 — Fact-Check Before Finalizing
Before presenting the final email, list every technology, tool, and metric used in the draft, and confirm each one appears verbatim (or is a direct, reasonable paraphrase) in the resume I supplied. Flag anything that isn't clearly supported instead of silently including it.

## Output
Provide:
1. **JD-to-Resume Match Summary** — match percentage, top 5 matching skills, top 3 matching experiences
2. **Fact-Check List** — every claim/metric in the draft mapped to its source in my resume
3. **Recommended Email Subject** — the strongest option
4. **Final Email** — one polished, copy-ready email
5. **Alternative Subject Lines** — 3 more options

The final email should read as a targeted, specific inquiry for this role at this company — not a generic job-seeking template.
