# Prompts-Library
Well-engineered AI Prompts for platforms like: ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini and other LLM-based AI assistants

A curated collection of high-quality, reusable AI prompts designed to help with career development, resume optimization, Data Engineering learning, interview preparation, and professional growth.

This repository contains structured prompts that can be used with AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and other LLM-based assistants to get more consistent, practical, and industry-oriented results.

## 🎯 Purpose

The goal of this repository is to create a centralized collection of well-engineered AI prompts that can be reused, customized, and continuously improved for different professional and learning scenarios.

Instead of asking AI tools generic questions, these prompts provide the necessary role, context, objectives, constraints, and expected output to guide the AI toward more relevant and actionable responses.

## ⭐ How to Use
  1. Navigate to the relevant category.
  2. Open the required .md prompt.
  3. Copy the prompt.
  4. Paste it into your preferred AI tool.
  5. Replace any placeholders with your own information.
  6. Follow the instructions provided by the prompt.
  7. Customize the prompt further based on your requirements.

## 💡 Why Use Structured Prompts?
  - A well-designed prompt can help AI:
  - Understand the user's background and goals
  - Maintain the required role and perspective
  - Produce structured and consistent responses
  - Avoid generic answers
  - Focus on industry-relevant practices
  - Generate actionable learning plans
  - Provide realistic interview scenarios
  - Improve resume and career-related outputs

The objective is not simply to create longer prompts, but to create better-contextualized prompts that produce useful results.
